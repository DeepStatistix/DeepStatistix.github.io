Research

Which forecast can we trust?

My research asks which model suits which series, how to measure accuracy honestly, and how to put forecasts and their uncertainty in front of the people who decide.

Flagship · HADP, SKUAST-Kashmir

Market Intelligence Cell (MIC)

A research programme and live platform for horticultural price forecasting under the Holistic Agriculture Development Programme, Government of Jammu & Kashmir. It publishes forecasts with ranges for growers, traders and agencies, plus an assistant that answers questions using figures taken straight from MIC's database.

78price series
25markets · 11 states/UTs
217ktrading-day observations
Visit micskuast.in →

What the research found

  • No universal winner. On the 2024 hold-out, 9 of 11 model families were best for at least one series.
  • Complexity is not a guarantee. The Transformer was best on one series and last on 35 of 78.
  • Live 2026 cherry season: model forecasts, reviewed before publication, had an error of 18.3% against 27.5% for the naive benchmark, winning on 11 of 13 series with 93% interval coverage.
  • PhD thesis: a custom stacking ensemble, tuned per market, gave the lowest error in all six apple markets (MAPE 1.1–3.0%).
